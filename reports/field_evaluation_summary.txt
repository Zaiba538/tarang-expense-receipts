when its image matched and both the clerk value and the OCR value are
present; blank values are counted in missing_reference / missing_ocr and
left out of the percentage, so it does not reflect how many fields are blank.

can be wrong. Photo-verified accuracy: reports/photo_field_evaluation.md.
